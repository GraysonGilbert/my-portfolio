## **Custom Modular Storage System: Ikea Billy Bookcase**

**Overview** To solve workspace clutter and organize shop hardware efficiently, I designed and manufactured a custom, 3D-printed sliding drawer system for the Ikea Billy Bookcase. Inspired by the open-source Gridfinity standard, this project replaces bulky wooden drawers and expensive commercial sliding hardware with a high-density, fully printable solution.

**Engineering & Design Highlights**

* **Custom Sliding Mechanism:** Engineered a fully 3D-printed sliding track system that utilizes small plastic BBs as low-friction rollers. Integrated neodymium magnets at the rear of the track act as a detent to securely hold the drawers closed.  
* **Standardized Modular Scaling:** Developed a variable-height drawer system indexed to the bookcase's existing shelf-pin holes. Drawer heights are standardized by unit increments (Small \= 1 unit, Medium \= 2 units, XXL \= 5 units), optimizing every inch of available vertical space.  
* **Digital Twin & Assembly Modeling:** Modeled a full-scale digital assembly of the bookcase and storage system in Fusion 360\. Testing tolerances and clearances in CAD prior to manufacturing saved significant print time and material waste.  
* **Iterative Enclosure Design:** Designed a flush-mount top enclosure featuring a single piece of laser-engraved acrylic with my personal logo. After prototyping several complex dual-articulating hinge mechanisms, I pivoted to a minimalist magnetic mounting system. This allows the panel to securely snap into place or be completely removed and stored magnetically on top of the unit.

**Takeaways & Future Iterations** Through extensive prototyping and assembly testing, this project strengthened my proficiency in Fusion 360\. While scaling the medium drawer into the small and XXL variants was successful, future iterations will utilize stricter parametric constraints to allow for seamless, formula-driven dimensional scaling across the entire system.
