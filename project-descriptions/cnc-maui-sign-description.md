## **CNC-Routed LED Sign: Maui, Hawaii**

**Overview** To build hands-on proficiency with end-to-end CAD/CAM workflows, I designed and manufactured a custom illuminated sign of the island of Maui. Machined on a Shapeoko 3 XXL CNC router, the project incorporates multi-tool operations, custom text engraving, and a recessed LED lighting system for a clean, flush aesthetic.

**CAD & Vector Workflow**

* **Vectorization:** Processed a reference outline of the island using Inkscape to trace the perimeter, embed typography, and generate a clean SVG vector file.  
* **3D Modeling:** Imported the 2D vector graphic into Fusion 360 as a baseline sketch. I smoothed the rough edge geometry and utilized offset and extrusion tools to define the physical body, outer contours, and internal channels for the lighting system.

**CAM & CNC Machining**

* **Toolpath Generation:** Programmed three primary manufacturing operations in Fusion 360, exporting the final G-code via Carbide Motion to cut the design from medium-density fiberboard (MDF) stock.  
* **Multi-Tool Execution:**  
  * **Engraving:** Executed the text detailing using a 90-degree V-carve bit.  
  * **Pocketing:** Cleared the internal trench for the LED strip using a 1/8” flat end mill.  
  * **Profiling:** Cut the final outer contour of the island using a 1/4” flat end mill.

**Hardware Integration & Final Assembly**

* **Flush Mounting:** Utilized a manual milling machine to cut precision recessed mounting points into the back of the MDF, allowing the sign to sit completely flat against a wall.  
* **Electrical Integration:** Press-fit the LED lighting strip into the CNC-routed pocket and soldered a barrel connector to interface cleanly with the external power supply.
