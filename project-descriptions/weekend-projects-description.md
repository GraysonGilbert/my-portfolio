## **Weekend & Rapid Fabrication Projects**

A collection of quick builds, targeted repairs, and rapid prototypes completed to solve everyday problems, support community initiatives, and repurpose shop materials.

### **Marina Dock Signs**

* **Overview:** Designed and produced high-visibility signage for slips and docks at the local neighborhood marina.  
* **Fabrication:** Created the vector layout and typography in LightBurn and cut the final graphics out of all-weather vinyl using a Cricut machine for clean, durable application to existing dock posts.

### **CNC-Machined Marina Wayfinding Sign**

* **Overview:** Following the success of the slip markers, I was commissioned to design a primary, weather-resistant entrance sign positioned to welcome boat traffic approaching the marina from the water.  
* **Design & Machining:** Developed the 2D artwork in LightBurn before transitioning the vector geometry into Fusion 360 to generate a full 3D CAD model. Machined from dual-color, UV-stabilized marine-grade sign plastic—revealing high-contrast lettering when engraved—and manufactured on an industrial CNC router.

### **Wing Foil High-Pressure Pump Adapter**

* **Overview:** When unable to source an off-the-shelf valve adapter to properly inflate a handheld wing for wing foiling, I designed and fabricated a custom solution.  
* **Fabrication:** Reverse-engineered the valve and hose dimensions to model a tight-tolerance, airtight nozzle adapter in CAD, then 3D printed the component for immediate, cost-effective field use.

### **Upcycled Laser-Engraved Wooden Coasters**

* **Overview:** Aiming to minimize scrap and maintain a low-waste shop workflow, I repurposed high-grade hardwood offcuts generated during my topographical laser-cut map production runs.  
* **Process & Tooling:** Engraved and cut geometric coaster designs on the makerspace laser cutter. To streamline batch assembly and ensure consistent alignment, I designed and 3D printed a custom press-fit alignment jig for adhering the non-slip cork backings.

### **'Chesupeake' Paddleboard Race Keychains**

* **Overview:** As an active participant in weekly summer paddleboard races on the Chesapeake Bay, I fabricated custom commemorative awards for top-finishing paddlers in the Chesupeake racing community.  
* **Fabrication:** Translated the organization's branding into vector artwork, then laser cut and engraved the logos onto batch-produced wooden keychain blanks as end-of-season podium prizes.

### **RTIC 1-Gallon Jug Air Vent Modification**

* **Overview:** Standard RTIC one-gallon water jugs suffer from poor pouring flow rates due to inadequate lid venting, leading to air-lock and slow bottle transfers.  
* **Engineering Solution:** After finding no commercial remedies, I designed an aftermarket venting plug in CAD and 3D printed the retrofit part. The integrated air vent eliminates internal vacuum during pouring, dramatically increasing flow rate and cutting bottle refill times down to seconds.
