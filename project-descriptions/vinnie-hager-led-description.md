## **Custom LED Art Installation**

**Overview**&nbsp;

Created as a gift for my mom, this project involved translating the intricate style of local artist Vinnie Hager into a physical illuminated art piece (completed with the artist's permission). Over the course of a summer, I meticulously hand-crafted the display, focusing on precision assembly and a clean, seamless aesthetic.

**Fabrication & Wire Management**

* **Manual Forming & Assembly:** Individually measured, cut, shaped, and bonded dozens of flexible LED segments by hand to precisely match the complex linework of the original artwork.  
* **Intricate Soldering:** Hand-soldered discrete power and ground connections for every individual light segment across the entire canvas.  
* **Concealed Routing:** Engineered a high-density wire management layout to completely hide the electrical routing from the front-facing view. This proved exceptionally challenging given the tight spatial constraints, with some of the larger continuous symbols requiring hidden interconnects for over 12 distinct LED segments.

**Takeaways & Design for Manufacturing (DFM)** While successfully completed entirely by hand, this project highlighted the critical value of automated manufacturing processes for complex geometries. In future iterations, I would transition from manual forming to a CNC-routed workflow. By modeling the design in CAD and machining precise recessed pockets into an acrylic backing board, the LED segments could be seamlessly press-fit into place. Implementing this standard commercial production method would dramatically reduce assembly time, simplify wire management, and guarantee exact geometric accuracy.
