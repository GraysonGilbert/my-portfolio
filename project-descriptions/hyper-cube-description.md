## **Infinity Mirror LED 'Hyper' Cube**

**Overview** Inspired by commercial infinity cubes, I engineered and fabricated this custom LED 'Hyper' Cube from scratch to build my proficiency in 3D modeling and additive manufacturing. The final piece integrates over 300 individually addressable LEDs and one-way mirrors to create a deep infinity illusion, fully managed via a Bluetooth smartphone controller.

**Mechanical Design & Fabrication**

* **Custom CAD Modeling:** Designed all structural and aesthetic components in Autodesk Fusion 360\. This included the primary frame connectors, interior mounting brackets, and specialized end caps to seamlessly conceal the raw edges of the outer aluminum trim.  
* **Hidden Wire Routing:** The core structure is supported by narrow acrylic tubing, which serves a dual purpose by acting as a concealed conduit for the complex wiring harness.  
* **Precision Assembly:** Due to extreme spatial constraints within the tubing and frame, every connecting wire had to be mapped and cut to exact lengths. This ensured a tight, neat form factor and prevented wire bunching from ruining the internal aesthetic.

**Electrical Integration & Troubleshooting**

* **Continuous Signal Flow:** Meticulously routed the data lines so the signal flows in a single, continuous direction around the entire cube. This topology ensures that dynamic lighting patterns and animations travel seamlessly in unison across all faces.  
* **Power Injection:** During initial testing, I encountered severe color accuracy degradation caused by voltage drop across the high volume of LEDs. I resolved this by implementing periodic power injection throughout the cube's matrix, ensuring uniform brightness and color fidelity across all 300+ diodes.  
* **Integrated Base Unit:** Engineered a custom base enclosure to neatly house the primary power supply, the Bluetooth LED controller, and the main power distribution wiring.
