## **Custom FPV Racing Drones**

**Overview** What began in 2016 as a creative outlet to capture dynamic aerial footage quickly evolved into a foundational passion for hardware and electromechanical systems. Designing, building, and piloting First-Person View (FPV) racing drones served as the primary catalyst for my engineering career, providing critical early exposure to hands-on fabrication, circuit integration, and complex system troubleshooting.

**Technical Skill Acquisition**

* **Electronics & Integration:** Developed a high level of precision soldering and wire management by assembling micro-electronics—including flight controllers, electronic speed controllers (ESCs), and video transmitters—within extreme weight and spatial constraints.  
* **Mechanical Design:** Utilized CAD modeling to design and fabricate custom structural components, camera mounts, and protective gear to optimize the airframe for durability and aerodynamics.  
* **System Diagnostics & Tuning:** Cultivated a rigorous approach to hardware troubleshooting. The continuous cycle of flying, crashing, and rebuilding required diagnosing complex electrical shorts, tuning PID loop flight characteristics, and optimizing radio frequency signal clarity.

**Impact & 2020 Build** Building and maintaining these high-performance systems instilled a deep appreciation for resilient hardware design and iterative problem-solving—ultimately driving my decision to pursue a formal engineering degree. The featured images highlight a custom quadcopter constructed during the 2020 lockdown, built from the ground up for agile freestyle flight and maximum durability.
